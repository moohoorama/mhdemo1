# Tall tree redraw

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. These replace the stretched-tree attempt with newly drawn trunks, branches and round foliage clusters. Original tree_0.png and tree_1.png remain as historical source art; runtime generation reads tree_0-redrawn.png and tree_1-redrawn.png.

Both selected source PNGs have genuine RGBA transparency, four horizontal animation cells, and are passed through the existing assetgen sampling/outline pipeline. Target body sizes: 14×24 and 12×20. The lower third of the trunk/root pixels is pinned to the first frame during preparation.

## tree_0-redrawn.png

A transparent PNG sprite sheet of four animation frames of one tall deciduous tree. Alpha background, isolated cutouts. Exactly four trees evenly spaced in one horizontal row on a 1536 by 768 canvas. Naturally tall tree anatomy: round layered olive green foliage clusters in upper 60 percent, visible forked brown trunk and roots in lower 40 percent, overall width to height ratio 0.58. Original chunky pixel art for a tiny isometric strategy game, readably simple shapes and 3 muted olive shades, illuminated from upper left. Same tree in four slight foliage sway poses, all trunks and roots remain identical in position. Safe margins. No text or shadows. Transparent background.

## tree_1-redrawn.png

A transparent PNG sprite sheet of four animation frames of one young deciduous tree, smaller companion to a tall mature tree. Alpha background, isolated cutouts. Exactly four trees evenly spaced in one horizontal row on a 1536 by 768 canvas. Natural tall sapling anatomy: THREE broad round foliage clusters, one large rounded crown at top and two offset smaller rounded clusters on left and right beneath, slender slightly bent brown trunk with one Y fork, modest roots. Crown in upper 60 percent and visible trunk in lower 40 percent. Overall width to height ratio 0.60. Original chunky pixel art for a tiny isometric strategy game, readable at 12x20 logical pixels; simple shapes, muted olive green palette, 3 foliage shades and 2 bark shades, illuminated from upper left, elevated three-quarter view. Same tree in four slight foliage sway poses: neutral, right, settled, left. All trunks and roots remain identical in position. Safe margins. No text or shadows. Transparent background.
